BunearyMusic — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026
Last Updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BunearyMusic (“the Bot”) collects, uses, stores, and handles information when you interact with the Bot through Discord.

We aim to collect only information necessary to operate the Bot and provide its features.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with BunearyMusic, the following information may be processed or stored.

1.1 Discord Information

We may process:

* Discord User IDs.
* Discord Server (Guild) IDs.
* Discord Voice Channel IDs.
* Discord account information made available through Discord’s API.
* Information associated with your interactions with Bot commands.

We use this information to identify users, manage access, provide music functionality, and operate the Bot.

1.2 Music and Listening Information

When you use music features, we may collect and store:

* Song titles.
* Artist names.
* Song URLs.
* Album artwork URLs.
* Number of songs successfully started from your requests.
* Your most-played songs and artists.
* Unique songs played.
* Listening time in the Bot’s voice channels.

These statistics are used to generate your personal music profile.

Other listeners are not credited with requesting a song simply because they were listening in the same voice channel.

1.3 Saved Playlists and Queues

If the relevant database features are configured and you use them, we may store:

* Playlist names.
* Playlist contents.
* Song titles and artists.
* Song URLs and playback metadata.
* Queue information.
* Discord User IDs associated with saved information.
* Discord Server IDs associated with saved queues.
* Save and update timestamps.

Saved playlists and queues are retained until deleted.

Automatic server resume queues may also be saved when the Bot leaves a voice channel.

1.4 Access and Moderation Information

BunearyMusic maintains access records that may include:

* Discord User IDs.
* First-interaction timestamps.
* Blacklist and whitelist status.
* Delegated owner-access status.
* Administrative reasons associated with access changes.
* Timestamps associated with moderation changes.

This information is used to manage Bot access and administrative permissions.

1.5 Technical and Operational Information

The Bot generates operational logs that may contain:

* Timestamps.
* Error messages.
* Command names.
* Server identifiers.
* Relevant user identifiers.
* Music provider connection information.
* Service availability and diagnostic information.

Sensitive credentials, including configured tokens and passwords, are redacted from application logs where supported.

We do not intentionally store your Discord password.

2. How We Use Information

Information collected by BunearyMusic is used to:

* Provide music playback functionality.
* Process music searches and requests.
* Maintain saved queues and playlists.
* Generate personal music profiles.
* Calculate listening statistics.
* Manage user access and permissions.
* Prevent abuse of the Bot.
* Diagnose technical problems.
* Maintain and improve Bot functionality.

We do not use your music statistics to create advertising profiles.

3. Third-Party Services

BunearyMusic relies on external services to provide certain features.

Discord

Discord provides the platform through which BunearyMusic operates.

Your interactions are subject to Discord’s Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Privacy Policy: https://discord.com/privacy

Lavalink Providers

BunearyMusic uses third-party Lavalink nodes through Shoukaku to process music playback.

The configured node provider may process information necessary to resolve and stream requested audio.

Different Lavalink providers may have different privacy practices and retention policies.

BunearyMusic does not operate or control every third-party Lavalink node.

Spotify

If Spotify integration is configured, BunearyMusic may communicate with Spotify’s API to retrieve music metadata.

This may include:

* Track titles.
* Artist names.
* Album information.
* Playlist information.

Spotify metadata is used to generate corresponding music searches.

BunearyMusic does not stream audio directly from Spotify.

Spotify Privacy Policy: https://www.spotify.com/legal/privacy-policy/

MongoDB

If database functionality is configured, MongoDB may be used to store:

* Personal playlists.
* Saved music queues.

These databases are separate from one another.

MongoDB’s privacy practices are subject to its applicable terms and policies.

4. Data Storage and Retention

BunearyMusic stores different categories of information separately.

Local Storage

Certain information is stored in the Bot’s local data directory, including:

* User access records.
* Listening statistics.
* Music profile information.

These files may persist between deployments if the hosting environment preserves the data directory.

Database Storage

When configured:

* Personal playlists are stored in a MongoDB database.
* Saved queues are stored in a separate MongoDB database.

Saved queues do not automatically expire. They remain stored until deleted.

Saved playlists remain stored until deleted.

Listening statistics and access records are retained until removed by the operator or through an available data-management process.

We do not currently promise a fixed automatic deletion period for these records.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not intentionally disclose personal information to unrelated third parties for advertising purposes.

Information may be processed by:

* Discord.
* Configured Lavalink providers.
* Spotify, when integration is enabled.
* MongoDB hosting providers, when database features are enabled.
* Infrastructure and hosting providers operating the Bot.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or necessary to protect the Bot and its users.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by BunearyMusic.

These include:

* Restricting administrative commands.
* Limiting access to owner-only functionality.
* Redacting configured credentials from application logs.
* Separating certain stored information into different databases.
* Using restricted local file permissions for supported data files.

However, no internet service or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

You should never share sensitive account information or credentials with the Bot.
